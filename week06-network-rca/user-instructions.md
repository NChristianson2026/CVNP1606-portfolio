Instructions for Fixing Your Network Connection

These steps will help you check your internet connection and fix common issues. Please follow them in order.



1\. Check if any internet works

Click the Start button in the bottom‑left corner of your screen.



Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).



Click in the address bar at the top and type:

www.microsoft.com



Press Enter.



If the page loads, your internet is working. If it does not load, continue to the next step.



2\. Restart your network connection

Click the Start button.



Type Settings and open it.



Click Network \& Internet.



Scroll down and click Advanced network settings.



Click More network adapter options.



A new window will open. Right‑click your Wi‑Fi or Ethernet connection.



Click Disable.



Wait 10 seconds.



Right‑click it again and click Enable.



This refreshes your connection and often fixes the issue.



3\. Run the Windows network troubleshooter

Click the Start button.



Type Troubleshoot settings and open it.



Click Other troubleshooters.



Find Internet Connections and click Run.



Follow the steps on the screen.

Windows will check your connection and try to fix the problem automatically.



4\. Reset your network settings to automatic

This step fixes the issue if your computer accidentally used the wrong IP address.



Click the Start button.



Type Control Panel and open it.



Click Network and Internet.



Click Network and Sharing Center.



On the left, click Change adapter settings.



Right‑click your network connection and choose Properties.



Click Internet Protocol Version 4 (TCP/IPv4) and then click Properties.



Make sure these two options are selected:



Obtain an IP address automatically



Obtain DNS server address automatically



Click OK to save the changes.



5\. When to stop and call the helpdesk

Please stop and call the ACME helpdesk if:



Websites still do not load after all steps



Your network icon shows a yellow warning triangle



The troubleshooter says it cannot fix the problem



You see an error message you don’t understand



Tell the helpdesk:

“I followed the network instructions and still cannot reach ACME resources.”
